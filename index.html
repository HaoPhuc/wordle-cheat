      How Wordfish rates a guess

      Chess.com runs Stockfish on every position, turns its evaluation into an expected score (roughly your chance of winning), and grades each move by how much expected score it threw away compared with the engine's best move. Wordfish does exactly the same thing, but the score is expected guesses to solve.

      The numbers

      • Eval: how many more guesses you should need from here (including this one) if you keep playing perfectly. Lower is better.
      • Eval bar: the expected total for this game on a 1–6 scale. Par is 4.
      • Bits: how much the guess shrinks the list on average (each bit halves it).
      • Left: answers you should expect to still have after this guess.
      • Win now / by next: chance this guess is the answer, and best chance of being done by the guess after.
